Lyric styles

One song.
Seven ways to watch it.

Pick a style in settings and switch any time. Colours can follow the album art, or you can choose your own.

Constellation

Each line becomes a constellation and each word a star. A thin line draws itself to the next star as it's sung.

Stack

Big bold words pile up in the middle. The word being sung sits in a rounded box.

Spotlight

One huge word at a time, punching in on the beat.

Karaoke

The line fills with colour left to right. The next line waits underneath.

Light wipe

Light sweeps through a dim line, word by word.

Type-on

Every word is typed out letter by letter, caret and all.

Underline

A glowing bar glides under the words as they're sung.

Make it yours

Five fonts (Sans, Rounded, Serif, Grotesk, Mono), text size, brightness, and a Clear, Dim or Black backdrop.

Edge light

A ribbon of light that runs with the song.

A soft light chases around the edges of your screen, breathes with the music's loudness, or just glows. It can match the lyrics or take any colour you like.

Run Breathe Still

Lock screen

Show your song off, phone style.

Press Ctrl + Alt + K for a full-screen display with the date, a big clock, the lyrics and a now-playing card. Any key or click closes it. It never asks for a password.

Settings

Everything in one window.

Click the star in your system tray. Choose the style, font, colours, screen, edge light, timing and shortcuts. Turn on Start with Windows and it's always there.

Shortcuts

Stays out of your way.

The lyrics are click-through, so you keep using your PC underneath. When the music pauses, they fade away.

Ctrl Alt L

Show or hide the lyrics.

Ctrl Alt K

Open or close the lock screen.

Ctrl Alt [ ]

Lyrics 0.1 s earlier or later, remembered for each app.

Tray star

Setup

Up and running in a minute.

Constella is one file. Nothing else to install, not even .NET.

Download Constella
  1. 1

    Download the zip

    Click Download. You get Constella-win-x64.zip (about 70 MB).

  2. 2

    Unzip it

    Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Move Constella.exe anywhere you like, such as Documents.

  3. 3

    Open Constella

    Double-click Constella.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info and then Run anyway. The first start takes a few seconds.

  4. 4

    Play a song

    Play something in Spotify or YouTube Music. The lyrics appear, and a star icon sits in your system tray for settings.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is Constella free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no sign-in.

Which music apps does it work with?

Anything that shows up in Windows' media controls (the now-playing card in the volume flyout): Spotify, YouTube and YouTube Music in Chrome or Edge, Apple Music, and many more.

Where do the lyrics come from?

From LRCLIB, a free library of synced lyrics. They're saved on your PC after the first play, so songs load instantly next time.

A song has no lyrics. What can I do?

Not every song is on LRCLIB yet. If you have a .lrc file for it, start the song, right-click the tray star and choose Load .lrc file. Constella remembers it for that song.

The words are a little early or late.

While the song plays, press Ctrl + Alt + [ to move lyrics earlier or Ctrl + Alt + ] to move them later. Each app keeps its own setting. There's also a Lyrics timing slider in settings for every song.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

Constella isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" for new apps like it. Click More info and then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

Does it listen to or record my audio?

No. For the Breathe edge light it reads how loud your PC's sound is, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The only thing it fetches online is lyrics.

Will it get in the way while I work or game?

No. The lyrics are click-through and fade away when music pauses. Press Ctrl + Alt + L to hide them any time.

How do I close or remove it?

Right-click the tray star and choose Quit. To remove it, delete Constella.exe. Your settings live in %APPDATA%\Constella if you want to clear those too.
